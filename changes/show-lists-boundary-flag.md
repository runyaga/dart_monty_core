### Internal

- Every export line of `dart_monty_core.dart` now has a `show` list naming exactly what it exports today; the public API is unchanged (`tool/export-golden.txt` is byte-identical). A name added to a file under `lib/src/` stays internal until it is listed.
- `ResourceLimits.validate`/`toProto` and `MontyCrashedError.exitCode`/`context` (both classes still internal) move into the extensions `ResourceLimitsInternal` and `MontyCrashedErrorInternal`, which the public `show` lists will leave out.
- `tool/check_export_boundary.dart --post-cutover` applies the boundary rule for after the cutover: a name declared under `lib/src/worker/` may be exported only when it is in the golden's names sections, and nothing from the protobuf, arena, channel, frame-scanner or wire-helper libraries may be exported or named in a public signature. Five new self-test controls cover it, plus one for the `show` lists. The `gate` CI job's timeout goes from 8 to 10 minutes.
- `tool/metrics_ratchet.sh` reads dcm's per-declaration results. A `number-of-parameters` violation listed in `tool/dcm-param-exceptions.txt` (empty for now; at most ten lines) is exempt; a stale or changed line fails. Under `RATCHET_BASE_REF`, regressions are compared with the base branch's baseline and the PR's own baseline must equal a fresh `--update`. `--self-test` (nine controls, no dcm needed) runs in the gate and CI.
